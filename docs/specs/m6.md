# M6 Managed launch

- **Milestone:** M6, weeks 24 to 28, after the 1.0 release (weeks 21 to 24; see `m5.md`)

Managed hosting on the same binary in multi-organization mode: the operator console above
organization admins (create, suspend and delete organizations; plans and grants; usage against
limits; non-profit applications), per-organization subdomains and custom domains with on-demand
ACME, quotas and rate limits per organization, plans as feature flags and limits checked by the
entitlements framework from M0, Stripe Checkout, the Customer Portal and Stripe Tax with
webhooks updating the plan, downgrades that make over-limit features read-only and never delete
data, the free non-profit plan renewed yearly, granted plans with an optional end date and an
audit trail, and the terms of service, privacy policy and DPA. Plan tiers and pricing are
deferred until this milestone. The UI work is the Managed hosting row (PlanBadge, UpgradePrompt,
UsageMeter, BillingSettings, SenderDomainSetup, OperatorConsole views). Billing gets the
maintainer's line-by-line review.

Plan: `docs/plan/technical.md` -> Plans and billing, Hosting and operations (Managed), Email and
notifications (Platform email), Deferred decisions (Plan tiers and pricing), Decided (Managed
hosting, Billing), Milestones; `docs/plan/ui.md` -> Component inventory (Managed hosting); ADRs
0006, 0007, 0008.

Full spec: written before M6 starts.
